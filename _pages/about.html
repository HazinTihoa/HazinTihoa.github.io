Zhihao Tian

I am an M.Phil. student in the Artificial Intelligence Thrust, Information Hub at HKUST (Guangzhou), supported by a full scholarship (2026–2028). I work with Prof. Junwei Liang at the Precognition Lab.

My research interests lie in robot learning, humanoid whole-body control, and motion planning. I am interested in building robots that learn useful skills and move effectively in the physical world.

Previously, I worked at the FAST Lab, Zhejiang University, with Dr. Yanjun Cao and Prof. Chao Xu. I received my B.Eng. in Mechanical and Electronic Engineering from Nanjing Institute of Technology.
