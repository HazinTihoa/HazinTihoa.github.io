Zhihao Tian

I am an M.Phil. student (fully funded) in the Artificial Intelligence Thrust, Information Hub at HKUST (Guangzhou) (2026–2028). I work with Prof. Junwei Liang at the Precognition Lab.

My research interests lie in robot learning, humanoid whole-body control, and motion planning. I am interested in building robots that learn useful skills and move effectively in the physical world.

Previously, I worked at the FAST Lab, Zhejiang University, with Dr. Yanjun Cao and Prof. Chao Xu. I received my B.Eng. in Mechanical and Electronic Engineering from Nanjing Institute of Technology.

I am a full-stack robotics engineer, and my longer-term ambition is to have my own robotics company. I believe in the long-term potential of robotics and hope to see robots help humanity become an interplanetary species within my lifetime. I look forward to that day with great excitement and am willing to do everything I can to help make it a reality.

Beyond research

I enjoy building things, from robot hardware to small everyday devices.

A robotic arm for a UAV

A servo-driven 5-DOF arm designed for mounting on a UAV, with a flexible gripper capable of grasping objects weighing 100 g. I reconstructed the design from figures in CHAT-PM because the original arm was not open-sourced.

Split Bluetooth keyboard

A split Bluetooth keyboard built from the open-source Corne design. It frees my hands from a fixed keyboard layout, although getting used to it takes some practice.

CubeTrack V1

I built this early CubeTrack prototype with Zelong Xia and Chice Xuan. Its compact 50 × 40 cm body could climb stairs at up to 1 m/s. The hardware and software still had plenty of flaws—and after one fast climb, the system crashed.

HoloCubic

My first college hardware project: a desktop display that uses a beam-splitter prism for a pseudo-holographic effect. It displays time, weather, and animations, and switches functions when its orientation changes.

original project ↗

Balance Car

A two-wheeled robot that uses an IMU and PID control to balance, with Bluetooth control from a smartphone.
